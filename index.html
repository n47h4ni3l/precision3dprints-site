// MATERIAL SPEC SHEET

Pick a material, or tell us the job

Not sure what you need? Choose "no preference" on the quote form and describe how the part will be used — we'll recommend one.

PLAGeneral purpose

Crisp detail, low warp, the default for models, fixtures and anything indoors.

PETGTough & durable

Better impact and moisture resistance. A strong all-rounder for functional parts.

ASAUV resistant

Holds up outdoors — brackets, housings and anything that lives in the sun.

TPUFlexible

Rubber-like flex for gaskets, grips, phone cases and vibration mounts.

ABSHeat resistant

Handles higher temperatures for engine-bay and enclosure applications.

CF / GFReinforced

Carbon- or glass-filled composites for stiffness where it actually matters.

// FAQ

Good to know

STL, STEP and 3MF are ideal. You can also send ZIP bundles if your model includes multiple parts.

Most quotes are returned within 12 hours. Print turnaround depends on size, material and queue — we'll confirm timing with your quote.

If you're not sure, choose "no preference" and tell us the intended use — we'll recommend the best option.

Shipping is free on every order, anywhere in Australia. If you're local and would rather skip the courier, we can sometimes arrange to meet up directly — the workshop itself isn't open to the public, so it's a meet-up rather than a shop visit.

Once you confirm you'd like to go ahead, we'll send an invoice. Payment's due before your part ships or is ready for pickup.

Basic checks and small printability suggestions are fine. For bigger CAD changes, include details in the notes and we'll tell you what's possible.

If a part has a quality issue that's on us — a print defect rather than something in the file you sent — we'll reprint it. Just get in touch and let us know.
