Materials

Material choice matters.

We choose materials based on heat, UV exposure, flex, strength, finish and intended use.

PLA

Clean and affordable

Good for decorative prints, indoor parts, display pieces and low-stress items.

PETG

Tougher everyday use

Better impact resistance and temperature tolerance for many practical parts.

ASA

Outdoor capable

Useful when UV and weather resistance are more important than the lowest price.

TPU

Flexible parts

For rubber-like covers, bushes, feet, pads and impact-absorbing pieces.

ABS

Heat capable

Useful for some stronger and warmer environments when the geometry suits it.

CF / GF

Stiffer composites

Carbon and glass-filled options for stiffness, finish and selected functional jobs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What file types do you accept?

STL, STEP and 3MF are best. ZIP folders are useful when you have multiple files or assemblies.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Most quotes are returned within 12 hours. Larger, risky or unusual jobs can take longer because the model needs to be checked properly.

Can you recommend a material?

Yes. Choose “No preference” on the form and tell us how the part will be used.

Do you offer pickup?

Local Adelaide pickup may be available by arrangement. This is a home workshop service, not a public walk-in shop.

Do you ship outside South Australia?

Yes, Australia-wide shipping is available.
